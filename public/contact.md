# Contact Jenergie

Jenergie does not use an online booking system. Contact Jenni directly to ask about sports massage, personal training or a bespoke exercise plan and to arrange an appointment.

- Email: Jen@jenergie.co.uk
- Phone: 07547 254349
- Location: Higham Ferrers, North Northamptonshire, near Rushden

In your message, briefly explain what you would like help with, which area feels tight or uncomfortable and the days or times that usually work for you. Jenni will reply to discuss the right appointment and confirm availability. A message is an enquiry until Jenni confirms the appointment.

The first sports massage appointment is 55 minutes. Tell Jenni about relevant injuries, health conditions or changes in how you feel. Jenergie provides sports massage and fitness support, not emergency or diagnostic medical care. For severe, sudden or worrying symptoms, seek appropriate medical advice.

Use the website contact form at https://jenergie.co.uk/contact/ or open https://tally.so/r/xXqDQJ. Name, email and message are required; phone is optional.
