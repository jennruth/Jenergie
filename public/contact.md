# Contact Jenergie

Jenergie does not use an online booking system. Contact Jenni directly to ask about sports massage, personal training or a bespoke exercise plan and to arrange an appointment.

- Email: Jen@jenergie.co.uk
- Phone: 07547 254349
- Location: Higham Ferrers, North Northamptonshire, near Rushden

In your message, briefly explain whether you are asking about sports massage, personal training or an exercise plan, along with the days or times that usually work for you. Please keep detailed medical and health information for your consultation. Jenni will reply to discuss the right appointment and confirm availability. A message is an enquiry until Jenni confirms the appointment.

The first sports massage appointment is 55 minutes. Relevant injuries, health conditions and changes in how you feel will be covered securely as part of your consultation, before treatment begins. Do not include detailed health information in your initial enquiry. Jenergie provides sports massage and fitness support, not emergency or diagnostic medical care. For severe, sudden or worrying symptoms, seek appropriate medical advice.
