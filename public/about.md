# About Jenergie

Jenergie is Jenni's sports massage practice in Higham Ferrers, close to Rushden and serving clients across North Northamptonshire. The main service is sports massage. People may visit because of tight or tired muscles after training, stiffness from work, an area that feels restricted or for regular maintenance. You do not need to be an athlete.

Before treatment, Jenni asks what you have noticed, what you want help with and whether anything may affect the appointment. She looks at how you move and plans the hands-on treatment around what is relevant that day. She explains what she is doing and may offer straightforward aftercare or movement suggestions.

One-to-one personal training and bespoke exercise plans are optional extras for people who want help with strength, exercise confidence or movement. They are not required as part of sports massage.
