# Jenergie frequently asked questions

These answers cover common questions about sports massage therapy and optional personal training with Jenni in Higham Ferrers, near Rushden. If you are unsure which appointment is right for you, contact Jenni before arranging a session.

## Do I need to be an athlete to have a sports massage?

No. People visit after exercise, because work or everyday routines leave them feeling stiff, or for regular muscular maintenance. Jenni will ask what you would like from the session.

## What happens at my first appointment?

The first sports massage appointment is 55 minutes. It begins with a conversation about how you are feeling and anything that could affect treatment. Jenni may look at how you move before the massage and will check that you are comfortable during the session.

## Which sports massage appointment should I choose?

The initial 55-minute appointment costs £45. Follow-up options are one area for 30 minutes at £35, or two areas for 60 minutes at £55. Ask Jenni if you are unsure which option fits your needs. See https://jenergie.co.uk/prices/ for current prices.

## Is personal training part of a massage appointment?

No. One-to-one personal training and bespoke exercise plans are optional extras. A one-hour training session costs £45, and a bespoke exercise plan costs £70. Neither is required for sports massage.

## How do I arrange an appointment?

Use https://jenergie.co.uk/contact/, email Jen@jenergie.co.uk or call 07547 254349. Jenergie has no online booking system. An appointment is confirmed only when Jenni has agreed the details with you.

## What if I feel unwell before my appointment?

Contact Jenni before attending. Treatment may need to be rearranged if you have an infectious illness, fever, vomiting or diarrhoea, or if treatment would not be appropriate that day. Read https://jenergie.co.uk/cancellation-policy/ for the full terms.

## What if I need to cancel or rearrange?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice where possible. A charge may apply for late cancellations or missed appointments. Exceptional circumstances may be considered. Read the full policy at https://jenergie.co.uk/cancellation-policy/.

## Where is Jenergie based?

Jenergie is based in Higham Ferrers, close to Rushden, and welcomes clients from across North Northamptonshire. Contact Jenni for appointment details.
