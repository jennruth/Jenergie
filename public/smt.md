# Jenergie Sports Massage Therapy

Jenergie provides tailored sports massage therapy from its appointment-based practice in Higham Ferrers, near Rushden. Clients are welcome from Wellingborough, Burton Latimer, Raunds, Podington, Irchester and across North Northamptonshire.

## What to expect

Sports massage begins with a conversation about what feels tight, tired or uncomfortable, how you move and what you would like help with. Treatment is adapted to you rather than following the same routine for everyone. You do not need to be an athlete. Sports massage can be useful for active people, physical workers and anyone whose daily routine leaves muscles feeling stiff.
