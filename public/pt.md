# Jenergie One-to-One Personal Training

Jenergie offers one-to-one personal training from its appointment-based practice in Higham Ferrers, near Rushden. Personal training is an optional service alongside sports massage and is available to clients from nearby towns across North Northamptonshire.

## Individual support

Sessions begin with a conversation about your goals, experience and preferences. Exercises are selected for your current level, with clear guidance on technique and progression. The aim is a practical approach that fits your routine rather than a generic programme.

## Prices

- One-to-one personal training: one hour, £45
- Bespoke exercise plan: £70
