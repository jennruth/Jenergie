## About Jenergie

Jenergie provides tailored sports massage therapy for muscular tension, stiffness, mobility, recovery and regular maintenance. You do not need to be an athlete to have a sports massage. Appointments take place in Higham Ferrers, close to Rushden, for clients across North Northamptonshire.

Personal training is offered separately for people who want one-to-one exercise support or a bespoke exercise plan. It is not required as part of a sports massage appointment.
